ABOUT US

Owned and operated by COE418 Entertainment, VOX Cinemas is the cinema arm of COE students, across the Middle East, Africa and Central Asia. CINX Cinemas is the Middle East’s most innovative and customer-focused exhibitor, and the fastest growing cinema business in the MENA region. With 57 locations totalling 573 screens across the region, including 237 screens in the Lebanon, 15 screens in UAE, 63 screens in Oman, 30 screens in Bahrain, 44 screens in Egypt, 149 screens in Saudi Arabia and 17 screens in Kuwait, VOX Cinemas is the Middle East’s largest and most rapidly growing exhibitor.

Tailoring every cinema location to the surrounding market place and customer preferences, CINX Cinemas offers a mix of experiences including luxury concepts, THEATRE and GOLD. Another signature CINX Cinemas concept, MAX is the mega-screen designed for the biggest blockbusters and features Dolby Atmos surround sound technology. CINX Cinemas was also the first to introduce IMAX with Laser to the Middle East and the only exhibitor to offer IMAX with Sapphire. The company also offers a multi-sensory movie-watching concept, 4DX. For young movie fans and families, CINX Cinemas offers the only cinema dedicated to children, KIDS. Complementing its portfolio of world-class experiences, CINX Cinemas offers OUTDOOR, an open-air concept.

Our Vision

Great Moments for Everyone, Everyday

The secret to our successful is threefold. Firstly, our intention is to meet the high expectations and standards set by Mr. Joe Tekli. His vision for all those businesses is to create great moments for everyone, every day. We aspire to fulfil this vision for every single customer every time they visit CINX Cinemas.

Secondly, our teams across the region are dedicated to creating exciting entertainment destinations that transport our guests from their ordinary lives into the extraordinary world of movies.

Lastly, the way we customise our cinemas to meet the expectations of our guests. Each cinema offers a wide variety of content and experiences.

CINX Cinemas features high-resolution digital projection systems, immersive digital 3D content, expansive MAX screens, Dolby Atmos and 7.1 multidimensional surround sound systems as well as premium experiences such as IMAX with Laser, THEATRE, GOLD, 4DX and KIDS, designed especially for our little movie fans.
